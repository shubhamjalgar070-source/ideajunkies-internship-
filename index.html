About the Company

Who is Idea Junkies?

A fast-growing AI consultancy based in the UK, helping businesses adopt AI safely, ethically, and practically — with people always at the centre.

Idea Junkies is a strategic AI consultancy founded by Laura Richards, a qualified AI & ML developer, Associate Lecturer at Newcastle University, and Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Public Relations. Their philosophy is simple: People First. AI Second. Always.

They are currently in a key growth phase, rapidly expanding their audience and building an engaged community around AI insights, tools, and trends. Their clients include Newcastle Building Society, Northumbrian Water, eToro, HiBob, and Clarion Housing.

The company holds a strong LinkedIn presence, an underused Instagram account, and no TikTok presence yet — which is exactly where this internship comes in.

Task 3 — Growth Idea

Ask Me If It's Hype

A weekly interactive series that turns audience anxiety into loyal, recurring engagement — and positions Idea Junkies as the trusted AI translator.

"Ask Me If It's Hype"

Every week, Laura invites followers to submit an AI tool, headline, or trend they've seen. She gives a straight, no-jargon verdict in 60 seconds. Three verdicts. One minute. Real talk.

🔥 It's Hype ✅ It's Helpful ⏳ Not Yet

MondayStory drops: "What AI thing confused you this week? 👇"
2
Thursday60-second Reel using real audience submissions — her honest verdict
3
Monthly"Most Ridiculous AI Hype" community poll — shareable, fun, human
4
As it growsGuest verdicts from HR directors, teachers, business founders
